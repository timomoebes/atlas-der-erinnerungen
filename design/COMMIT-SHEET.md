# Art Direction · Atlas der Erinnerungen

1. **Moment of care:** A memory appears exactly where it belongs on the Berlin–Paphos flight path, and the presentation mode makes the family story readable together.
2. **Color:** Restrained product shell, cool off-white `oklch(0.965 0.008 220)` (mean page L target 0.72); chart-ocean `oklch(0.29 0.055 230)`, coral `oklch(0.72 0.16 35)`. Not cream or lavender: sea, flight charts, and Mediterranean sunset are actual places in the story.
3. **Type:** Fraunces for intimate editorial headings, DM Sans for legible controls and diary prose. Not Inter: this needs the texture of a family album rather than enterprise software.
4. **Grid break:** A large rounded map tableau takes most of the width, with a narrower editorial rail and a floating place label over the globe.
5. **Motion:** A slow orbiting plane tells the journey; short 160–220 ms controls and panel transitions. No scroll choreography. Reduced-motion freezes the aircraft.
6. **Reflex check:** First-order travel site: pastel map, identical cards, postcard photos. Second-order: near-black luxury map with gold chips. Instead: daylight editorial shell around one richly lit midnight-teal globe, with archival typography and tactile route markers.
7. **House tells broken:** No dark body by default; no mono micro-label status bar; no wordmark hero. The mapped journey is the central object.

## Product sheet
- Main atlas: map, journey, memory timeline, selected entry. Empty state guides first note.
- Editor dialog: create/edit title, date, route position, note, photograph; save/error states.
- Presentation overlay: one memory at a time, keyboard navigation, family viewing.
- Shared controls: primary/quiet/danger buttons; selected timeline item; field/focus/error states. No accounts. Data stored in IndexedDB on this browser. JSON export/import is the portable handoff.
